Brand style guide · Final artwork · November 2026

The essentials, on one page.

The final Cipher & Veil Advisory logo, its colours and its type, with the rules that keep it looking the same on a website, a slide, a letterhead or a trade-show wall. Every file referred to here is in the brand assets folder.

Prepared for Aveen Sufi · Skyway Media

01

Logo

The primary logo is a horizontal lockup: the CV mark, drawn as one continuous stroke, beside the name in spaced serif capitals with “Advisory” centred beneath. It is supplied as finished artwork. Always place a supplied file; never retype, redraw or rebuild it.

Which version
  • Full colour on white, paper or sand. This is the default.
  • Reversed (sand mark, paper name) on evergreen or ink.
  • One colour when only a single ink is available: embroidery, engraving, stamps, one-colour print.
  • On photographs, use white or reversed over a dark, quiet area of the image.
Which part
  • Logo first, wherever it fits.
  • Mark alone where the space is square or tiny, and where the name already appears nearby.
  • Wordmark alone where the mark would be too small to read, or in centred layouts.
  • Never pair the mark and wordmark in a new arrangement of your own.
Clear space
Keep x clear on every side

x is the height of a capital letter in the name. Keep at least that much empty space around the whole logo: no text, no other logos, no edge of the page or image inside it. More is better. The same rule applies to the mark and the wordmark on their own, using half the height of the mark as x for the mark.

Contrast ratios follow WCAG 2.1: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and graphics. Screen values are given in HEX and RGB. For print, have the printer match to these values or to a Pantone swatch chosen from a physical proof; CMYK conversions vary by press and paper.

03

Typography

Two families, both open-licence and free to self-host, which keeps the website clear of third-party font requests. The logo itself is outlined artwork and needs no font installed.

Instrument SerifHeadlines, pull quotes, the tagline. Regular and italic only.
A different perspective. Earned from the inside.
Cipher & Veil

Sentence case for headlines. Spaced capitals are reserved for the name and very short titles. Never bold: the family has one weight, and faked bold distorts it.

Instrument SansBody copy, captions, buttons, forms, tables. 400, 500, 600.

Cipher & Veil builds AI governance, data privacy and third-party risk programs for companies that have found themselves regulated. Body text is set in Ink at 16 px or larger on screen, 10 pt or larger in print, with a line height around 1.5 and lines no longer than about 70 characters.

Regular for reading, medium for emphasis, semibold for small headings and buttons.

LabelsInstrument Sans 600, capitals, wide tracking.
Advisory · Privacy · AI governance

Echoes “Advisory” in the logo. Keep to a few words; 11 to 13 px with letter-spacing of about 0.2 to 0.35 em.

FallbacksWhen the brand fonts are unavailable.

Georgia in place of Instrument Serif; Arial or Helvetica in place of Instrument Sans. Use these in email and in shared Office documents where fonts cannot be embedded.
