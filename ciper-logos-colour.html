Cipher & Veil — Lockups in colour

The four horizontal lockups, final artwork, on every ground in the Evergreen & Stone palette. The mark takes evergreen on light grounds and sand on dark; the wordmark takes ink on light and paper on dark. On stone the mark drops to ink, since evergreen on stone only passes for large sizes.
