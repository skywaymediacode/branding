Brand Archetype

Foundation

Integrated Wellness Institute occupies a composed archetype: Clinical Trustworthy, warmed. The clinical half is non-negotiable — doctorate-level course developers, NBCC ACEP approval, evidence-based curriculum — but the classic clinical aesthetic (cool blues, sterile sans-serifs, hospital register) would misrepresent a brand whose subject is human resilience. Sage & Gold warms the archetype: sage carries the clinical calm, gold carries the human reward. Imagery follows a Documentary Honest direction throughout.

Tone Register
ConversationalProfessional

Professional, softening toward conversational only on individual-facing pages. Never casual with agencies; never cold with individuals.

Aesthetic Register
RestrainedExpressive

Restrained-to-considered. Gold is the only expressive note, and it is rationed — one accent per composition.

Audience Relationship
CompanionAuthority

Authority with agencies and procurement; peer with licensed clinicians; guide with individuals. Never companion-cute.

Sensory Ambition
UtilitarianTheatrical

Considered. Quality signaled through spacing, hierarchy, and warm neutrals — never through effects or drama.

Adaptation choices, flagged. Three deliberate deviations from archetype defaults: (1) the Clinical Trustworthy palette is warmed from blue-grey to sage to accommodate the fixed warm-grey ring mark; (2) type is serif-led (Cinzel) rather than sans-led, honoring the inscriptional wordmark; (3) the audience axis holds at Authority despite the wellness vertical's usual drift toward Companion, because the primary buyer is institutional. The archetype to actively avoid is Rugged Utilitarian — no tactical black, OD green, coyote tan, or thin-line motifs, ever.

Logo System

The Mark

The identity is a three-tier logo system built on one fixed geometry: three interlocking rings above the Cinzel wordmark. The rings' shape, stroke weight, and overlap never change — only their color, and only per the tiers below.

IntegratedWellness Institute
Primary MarkHeritage grey rings #B9B1AD · Deep Sage wordmark. Default everywhere: website header, documents, certificates, signage.
IntegratedWellness Institute
Tonal MarkSage-tint rings #B5BDB9. For fully sage-toned layouts where the heritage grey would read as a mismatch. Interchangeable with primary; never mix both in one piece.
IntegratedWellness Institute
Accent MarkTri-color rings: Deep Sage · Sage · Gold, left to right. Secondary use only — course badges, social avatars, certificates of completion, celebratory moments. Never the website header or formal documents.
IntegratedWellness Institute
Reversed MarkOn Evergreen or photography: sage-tint rings, Linen wordmark. Gold wordmark is permitted on Evergreen for celebratory pieces only.

Clearspace & Minimum Size

Keep clear space on all sides equal to half the height of the ring artwork (X = ring height ÷ 2). Nothing enters this zone — no text, rules, photography edges, or other marks. Minimum sizes: full lockup 120px wide on screen / 30mm in print; rings-only mark 24px / 8mm.

Sage & Gold

Color

Sage is the brand's clinical calm; gold is its human reward. The system is built so sage does the talking and gold does the pointing — gold appears once per composition, on the element that matters most.

Cinzel & Jost

Typography

Cinzel is the brand voice at display size — Roman inscriptional capitals that match the wordmark, reserved for the logo, page titles, and section headings. It is always set in caps with tracking of 0.04–0.10em, never in running text. Jost is the workhorse: body copy, UI, captions, tables, forms. Its geometric warmth keeps dense content approachable without competing with Cinzel's formality. Both are free Google Fonts.

How IWI Speaks

Voice & Tone

The voice is declarative, numeric, and calm — an experienced clinician briefing professionals, never a wellness brand soothing consumers and never a tactical brand hyping operators. Claims are countable; urgency is absent; trauma is named clinically, not dramatized. This also keeps advertising inside Google's health-policy lines: advertise the credential, never the condition.

Documentary Honest

Imagery

Photography shows real people doing real work in natural light — a dispatcher mid-shift, a firehouse kitchen conversation, a training room in use. Muted warm grade that sits on Linen without fighting sage or gold. No posed hero stances, no flag-and-eagle montages, no tactical costume, no stock-photo gloss, and no imagery that dramatizes crisis or trauma. When people appear, they are working or talking — never staring meaningfully into the middle distance.

Treatment: warm highlights, soft green-grey shadows, gentle contrast. Duotone treatments, when needed for covers, use Evergreen shadows with Linen highlights — never gold duotones over faces.

Never Do This

Misuse
  1. Alter ring geometry, stroke weight, spacing, or add a fourth ring
  2. Set the wordmark in any face other than Cinzel, or in lowercase
  3. Use gold for body text on light backgrounds (2.2:1 — fails)
  4. Mix heritage-grey and sage-tint rings in the same piece
  5. Use the tri-color accent mark on formal documents or the site header
  6. Place the mark over busy photography without a scrim
  7. Introduce tactical black / OD green / coyote tan or thin-line motifs
  8. Use more than one gold accent per composition
  9. Write trauma-forward hooks or urgency copy ("spots filling fast")
