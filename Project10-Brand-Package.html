Primary logo

The horizontal lockup is the default. The tree, owl and wordmark are locked in proportion — never re-space or re-scale the parts independently. Keep clear space of at least the height of the “P” on all four sides.

Icons

Two marks carry the brand where the full lockup will not fit. Both come from the primary logo, so nothing new is introduced by using them.

Tree mark

The lead icon. Use for app icons, favicons, social profiles and anywhere the logo appears without the name.

Owl mark

The accent icon. Use as a bullet, watermark, stamp or section marker — not as a replacement for the logo.

Colour palette

Using it well

A short list that keeps the identity consistent wherever it is applied.

Do

Place the logo on white, mist or a light photograph area with room to breathe.

Do

Use the tree mark alone when space is tight — profile pictures, favicons, stamps.

Do

Keep sage as the dominant brand colour, with lime reserved for accents.

Don’t

Recolour, rotate, stretch or add effects — no shadows, outlines or gradients.

Don’t

Place the logo on a busy image or a dark background without a reversed version.

Don’t

Rebuild the wordmark by typing it — always use the supplied artwork.
