Brand palette expansion

More range.
Same trusted identity.

A flexible supporting color system designed to preserve the established logo while creating a calmer, more modern and more useful visual language across web, social, print and patient materials.

01 · Existing foundation

Current logo colors remain unchanged

These working digital values were sampled from the raster logo, alongside the values the live site CSS actually uses (Brand Blue #005EB8, heading red #DC4534, CTA red #F20000). They anchor every direction below; new colors are for the surrounding brand system, never for the logo itself.

02 · Palette directions

Four ways to expand the system

The brand sits between two aesthetic families: a clinical, trustworthy backbone (cool blues, clean structure, medical credibility) and playful, energetic accents (the mascots, the brights). Each direction below extends one side of that blend — every value was contrast-checked against WCAG 2.1. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Recommendation

Direction 1 as the backbone, Direction 3 as the accent layer. The blue range gives the site a calmer, more credible foundation without introducing a single new hue, while the teal-and-green accents formalize colors the mascot icons already use. Directions 2 and 4 layer in as needed for children's materials and neutrals.

Direction 01 · Clinical backbone

Calm Confidence — The Blue Range

A full light-to-dark tonal range of the existing Brand Blue. The pale tints replace flat white and gray section backgrounds; the dark ends give headlines and the footer more weight — all without adding any new hue.

Preferred route

Direction 02 · Playful warmth

Warm & Pediatric — Red & Yellow Tones

Softer, warmer versions of the logo's red and yellow so those colors can appear at large scale without feeling alarming — important in a medical setting for kids. Brick replaces the current harsh #790000 for red text.

Direction 03 · Accent layer

Fresh Accents — Teal, Green & Orange

The homepage mascots already use a teal nurse-bear and a green croc — this direction formalizes those hues and adds orange as a bridge between the logo's red and yellow. Each service area or mascot can own a color.

Direction 04 · Quiet support

Calm Accents & Refreshed Neutrals

A calm purple family for the Learning Center, plus blue-cast neutrals that replace today's flat #999999 / #666666 grays so even the quiet colors feel like part of the brand.

03 · Application guidance

Let the brighter logo colors work as accents

A restrained distribution keeps the brand credible and easy to navigate while preserving its recognizable pediatric energy.

Recommended color distribution

65% 20% 10% 5%
  • 65%White, Ice, Powder, Mint, Cream and Lavender backgrounds
  • 20%Deep Navy and Charcoal structural colors
  • 10%Brand Blue plus the Teal / Green / Purple accent layer
  • 5%Red, Coral, Orange and Yellow highlights

Practical rules

  • Keep the existing logo artwork and its colors unchanged.
  • Use Deep Navy for major headings, navigation and footer areas.
  • Use Brand Blue for primary calls to action and Deep Teal for secondary actions.
  • Reserve red for urgency and small emphasis; use Brick #A8352A whenever red must be read as text.
  • Use yellow, coral and orange for highlights and graphic details — never for body copy.
  • Use the pale tints (Ice, Powder, Blush, Cream, Mint, Lavender, Fog) to separate sections; all support Navy or Charcoal text at AAA.
Accessibility: Safe text-on-white colors are Deep Navy, Deep Blue, Brand Blue, Brick, Deep Teal, Deep Green, Purple, Charcoal and Slate (all ≥ 4.5:1). The current heading red #DC4534 and CTA red #F20000 only pass at large sizes (≥ 3:1); Sunshine Yellow, Sky, Coral, Orange, Teal and Grass Green are decorative — pair them with Navy or Charcoal type. Ratios computed per WCAG 2.1.
