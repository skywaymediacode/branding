The mark

A medium cut at half that gain sits between them for the 32–48px range, where the standard hairline is starting to thin but the small cut looks heavy. It is drawn on a square canvas for favicons, app icons and social avatars, with a light and a reversed version.

03

Evergreen & Stone, refined

Evergreen moves to #1D3A2D: a touch more saturated, a touch deeper, and it slightly improves every contrast pairing it is part of. Stone is a surface and rule colour, not a text colour on light grounds: it fails against paper and white, passes for large text on evergreen, and passes as a ground under ink. The table shows every pairing in the palette against WCAG AA.

Where stone goes. Section backgrounds under ink text, hairline dividers, inactive icons and secondary buttons, the “muted” tier of a UI. Never body or label text on paper or white.
Where sand goes. The mark and wordmark on evergreen or ink, surface tints, rules and highlights. Sand as text passes only on evergreen and ink, and both pass AA comfortably.
Text on light grounds is ink or evergreen, both well past AA on paper and white. Evergreen on stone passes for large text only, so headlines on a stone band are fine; body copy on stone is ink.

05

Fonts, hosting & privacy

Confirmed: every font on the production site will be served from the site’s own domain. No request to fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, Adobe Fonts or any other third-party font service, so no visitor IP leaves the site to load type. The checklist below is what we verify before launch; this review page itself still loads from Google for convenience and is not the production setup.

Font files on the server. WOFF2 files for every weight and style in use, uploaded to the theme, declared with @font-face and preloaded. Nothing fetched at runtime from a CDN.
Theme audit. The theme’s own Google Fonts enqueue is disabled or removed in the child theme; the typography settings point at the self-hosted families.
Builder settings. If a page builder is in play, its “load Google Fonts” option is turned off and custom fonts are registered inside it so every text style resolves locally.
Plugin sweep. Every active plugin is checked for font, icon-font or embed requests to third-party hosts; offenders are replaced or their remote loading is switched off.
Network proof. Before launch, a full-site crawl of outgoing requests confirms zero calls to font hosts. The report is kept as evidence of the control.
Privacy page. The site’s privacy notice states that fonts are self-hosted, which is a small, true thing to be able to say for a governance brand.
