Cipher & Veil — Round 2: Two marks

Round 1 narrowed to two directions. This round shows them side by side at the sizes they will actually live at, with the two typefaces and the two colour directions that made the cut. Numbering and letters carry over from Round 1 so nothing has to be renamed.

October 2026 · Prepared for Aveen Sufi

01

Marks

Each mark on white, reversed on ink, and then at avatar, favicon and footer sizes. The two take opposite routes to the same brief: 02 is one confident stroke, 03 is a sequence of fine ones. Judge them at the small sizes first; that is where the difference shows.

02The Insider

One continuous stroke, C into V, signed off with a tail. A person’s hand on the work rather than a firm on the letterhead. Holds its weight at every size; the tail is the only detail that softens below 24px.

Why this pairing
Drawn as one family, so the serif and the sans share proportions and never fight. The serif is the estate; the sans is the person who runs it. Fraunces stays available as the headline face if the warmer voice wins, with Instrument Sans unchanged underneath it.
Wordmark

Descriptor

Minimums
Wordmark 18px on screen, 7mm in print; body 16px, never below 14px
03

Colors

Two directions remain. Both keep Ink #14161F and Sand #DCC7A0 from the brief and add one depth colour, one paper and one muted tone. Nothing blue.

B · Oxblood & Parchment

The red-into-purple gradient from the assessment read as temperament rather than palette: one deep oxblood in the ground, never rose. The more luxurious of the two; the trade-show direction.

Ink#14161F
Sand#DCC7A0
Oxblood#512A32
Parchment#F4EEE6
Taupe#9E8D7B

C · Evergreen & Stone

A deep green as the second ground reads as established and quietly expensive without borrowing the legal category’s navy. Stone keeps the operational pages calm. The more “estate” of the two.

Ink#14161F
Sand#DCC7A0
Evergreen#293C34
Paper#F3F0E7
Stone#969B8D
Text on light grounds uses Ink or the palette’s depth colour, never Sand. Sand on white fails contrast at any text size, so it stays a mark and surface colour.
Avoided, by the brief: Cobalt #2660B8, Violet #5B4A9E, Rose #D0697F. No foil, no marble, no gold gradient in the system; sand reads as light, not as gold.
Resemblance check: black is Deloitte’s ground and black-and-cream is old Big Law. Sand used generously and warmly is what separates the brand from both.
04

Combinations

Each mark with each typeface, in title case and in capitals, on both grounds of each colour direction. Sixteen lockups per mark. Ink reversals are in section 01; these show the two depth colours doing the work.
