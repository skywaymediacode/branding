Four concepts · Three voices · Two grounds

Twenty four lockups.

Each concept runs across one row, set as a horizontal lockup. The mark sits on the left with the letters beside it at matching size, and the tagline holds the line underneath. Each typeface takes its own line now, on white at the left and reversed on black at the right, so you can judge a pairing in one glance.

A note on CareSans. It is a licensed face rather than a hosted one, so this page calls it from your machine. When it is installed you will see the real thing. When it is not, Figtree stands in so the lockup still holds its shape.
01The figure in the coin. A drawn form held inside a solid circle, motion first, symbol second.
02The ribbon knot. One band folds back on itself inside the coin and settles on a single point of rest.
03The open O. One line draws the whole letter, then sweeps out of frame.
04The loop. No fill and no container, just a continuous line finding its way around.
