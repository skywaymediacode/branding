02

Bricolage and the italic question

Correct: Bricolage Grotesque ships upright only. A browser will fake a slant if asked, and the result is the first box below: it is what you get by accident, and it is not good enough for a brand that cares about craft. The other three boxes are the real options. Our recommendation is the second: Bricolage for everything upright, and the serif italic for emphasis. It is a long-standing editorial convention, it uses a face already in the system, and it keeps the Ruler-and-Everyman split visible even inside a paragraph.

03

GT America and the Typewolf ten

Typewolf’s list of the ten most-used sans serifs on the web, with GT America at number three. Every one of them is a licensed commercial face, so none can be shown on this page as itself; each row is set in the closest open face as a stand-in, named on the right, with the foundry and whether the licensed original has italics. If any of these wins, the same rule as GT Sectra applies: trial files for a true comp, then a web licence, self-hosted. The three faces we would shortlist from this list for Cipher & Veil are GT America, Founders Grotesk and Neue Haas Grotesk; the geometric and rounded ones read as consumer brands.
