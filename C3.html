01

Arc endings

These are not three different drawings — they are one drawing with one number changed: the radius at each terminal.

Flat

Cut square across the stroke.

Softened

The corners taken off — about a fifth of the stroke weight.

Rounded

A full semicircle. The radius equals half the stroke.

02

Colour order

Six arrangements on bone. Navy dominant in every one, light blue nowhere.

On dark — secondary

Navy cannot appear inside the mark on a navy ground, so the dark colour way inverts: bone and stone carry the arcs and copper stays the accent.

03
